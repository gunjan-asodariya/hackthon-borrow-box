About this adventure vehicle

Escape into the North Georgia Mountains, explore the Blue Ridge Parkway, or set out on a multi-state overland road trip with this fully equipped 2024 Ford Bronco Badlands 4x4 Overland Edition. Built for performance both on-highway and off-grid, this rig combines rugged capability with modern comfort and safety features.

Powered by the heavy-duty 2.7L EcoBoost V6 (330 HP / 415 lb-ft torque) paired to a 10-speed automatic transmission, outfitted with the Sasquatch Package (factory 35" mud-terrain tires, Bilstein position-sensitive dampers, electronic-locking front and rear axles), high-clearance fender flares, heavy-duty recovery gear, 12,000 lbs WARN winch, integrated Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, Bang & Olufsen 10-speaker sound, and a 3,500 lbs factory tow package.

Every vehicle is thoroughly mechanically inspected, sanitized, and fully fueled prior to pickup or delivery. Complete with off-road emergency recovery boards, an onboard 12V air compressor, and a heavy-duty integrated cargo slider for easy gear access. Perfect for outdoor enthusiasts, campers, and film crews.
