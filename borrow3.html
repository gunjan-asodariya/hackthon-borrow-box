About this venue

Welcome to The Glasshouse Rooftop & Garden Terrace, an extraordinary venue design for unforgettable milestone celebrations, corporate mixers, intimate wedding receptions, and high-end brand activations.

Spanning over 3,200 square feet of seamlessly connected indoor and outdoor space in Chicago's vibrant West Loop district, this property features custom architectural glass enclosure with full climate control, retractable roof sections, ambient lounge seating, custom outdoor fire pits, and unimpeded skyline views of downtown Chicago.

Flexible layout capabilities allow catering setups for seated meals or cocktail formats. Our site manager remains on-site during event hours to assist with technical setup, lighting presets, and sound equipment orientation.

Included in venue party booking:

  • Pro Sound System: Dual QSC powered speakers, wireless mic & Bluetooth AUX mixer
  • Modular Mobile Bar: Custom walnut wood bar setup with ice well & speed rail
  • Catering Prep Kitchen: Commercial ice maker, refrigerator, warmers & prep tables
  • Furniture & Seating: 8 Lounge sofas, 6 high-top bar tables & 30 folding chairs
  • Atmospheric Lighting: Dimmable Bistro lights, RGB color wash LED, & candle lanterns
  • On-Site Support Staff: Venue host & dedicated technician for duration

Event Rules & House Guidelines

• Music Curfew: Outdoor speakers amplified sound lowers at 10:00 PM; indoor sound allowed until 12:00 AM.

• Decorations: Sticking items on glass requires low-tack painter's tape; no open glitter or confetti allowed.

• Setup & Teardown: Your booked hours must include setup and teardown time needed.
