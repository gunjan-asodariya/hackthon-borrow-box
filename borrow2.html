About this ride

Looking to tour the scenic DuPage River trails, run errands around Naperville without any traffic, or test-drive an e-bike before buying? The Specialized Turbo Vado 4.0 is known as the "Swiss Army Knife" of urban electric bikes. Smooth power delivery, comfortable geometry, and modern integrated lights make this a dream ride.

Equipped with the ultra-efficient Specialized 2.0E motor (50Nm torque) and an integrated 710Wh battery, this setup yields up to 90 miles of effortless range in Eco mode (40–50 miles in high-power Sport/Turbo mode). It comes equipped with 80mm front suspension travel to smooth out pavement cracks and gravel multi-use paths.
