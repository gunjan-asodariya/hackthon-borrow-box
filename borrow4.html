About this modular sofa set

Elevate your temporary living space or luxury home video/film shoot staging presentation with this iconic Restoration Hardware style 6-piece deep cloud modular sectional. Finished in a warm ivory stain-resistant textured linen profile, fits seamlessly into contemporary, modern organic, or modern farmhouse interiors.
